♪ MUSIC ROOM ♫

A first-person browser toy. Walk up to a shelf of five instruments, pick one up, and play it from your keyboard. All graphics and sound are generated in code.

Overview

You stand in a small music room lit by a neon sign. On the counter in front of you are five instruments under spotlights. You can see your own arms, and they react to what you do.

🎯 Pick

Point at an instrument with the mouse, or move between them with the arrow keys. Your right hand follows the cursor.

🎶 Play

Eight keys on the home row play a full C-major octave, or eight drum sounds on the drum kit. The instrument and your hands move as you play.

🔁 Swap

Put the instrument back, or press 1–5 to change instruments directly. The game handles the put-down and pick-up for you.

No asset files. Every sprite is drawn on a canvas and every sound is made with the Web Audio API, so the whole game is just index.html, style.css and three scripts.

Any keyboard layout works. Note keys are read by physical position (KeyA…KeyK). On browsers that support the Keyboard Map API, the on-screen pads show your real key caps, for example Q instead of A on AZERTY.

Game loop

The game has one main state, the hold progress p, which goes from 0 (on the shelf) to 1 (in your hands). Each transition takes 0.55 s with an ease-in-out curve.

1 · BrowseThe focused instrument bobs, glows and gets a brighter spotlight. A tooltip shows its name and description.
→
2 · Pick upThe instrument arcs up off the shelf into your hands. The room darkens and the browsing hint fades out.
→
3 · PlayInput opens once p > 0.85. The note pads, the instrument name and the "put back" hint appear.
→
4 · Put backAll notes stop and the instrument returns to its slot. Then you are back to browsing.

Swapping instruments

Pressing 1–5 while holding an instrument puts the current one back first. The new choice is remembered as pending and is picked up automatically once the first one is on the shelf. Pressing the number of the instrument you are already holding does nothing.

Changing your mind

If you press the number of the instrument you are putting back, it comes back to your hands. Every pick-up starts with fresh instrument effects (strings still, valves up, bow centred).

Feedback & feel

Floating notes

Each note sends up two ♪/♫ symbols in the instrument's colour, from the sound hole, bell or drum head. They drift and fade out over about 1.4 s.

Note pads

The top HUD lights up the pad in the instrument's colour while its note is held or ringing. Pads show the key cap and the note or drum name.

Living camera

While browsing, the view sways slightly toward the mouse and the walls move with parallax. A slow breathing bob runs all the time. A held instrument sways a little in your hands.

First-person arms

Browsing: your right hand points at the cursor and your left hand rests. Holding: both arms rise into a pose for that instrument (grip, point, open or stick).

Room lighting

Spotlights brighten over the focused slot and its brass name plate turns gold. Holding an instrument puts a vignette over the room. The neon sign flickers now and then.

Custom cursor

The system cursor is hidden and replaced with a small circular reticle drawn on the canvas.

Audio engine

js/audio.js exposes a small Sound module with init() and play(id, noteIndex).

Signal chain

voice → out ─┬─────────────→ master (0.7) → compressor → speakers
             └→ reverb (1.6 s) → wet (0.25) ─┘

The reverb impulse is random noise generated at start-up. A dynamics compressor keeps chords and drum rolls from clipping.

Voices

  • One-shot (guitar, drums): play returns null and the sound rings out by itself.
  • Sustained (keyboard, violin, trumpet): play returns { stop() }, which fades the gain out smoothly to avoid clicks.
  • Guitar string buffers are cached per pitch, so they are only computed once.
  • The audio context starts on the first click or key press, as browsers require.

Technical notes

Project files

  • index.html: the canvas and script tags
  • style.css: centres the canvas and hides the system cursor
  • js/draw.js: room, shelf, instruments, arms and HUD helpers
  • js/audio.js: synthesis and the effects chain
  • js/main.js: instrument definitions, state, update and render loop, input

Rendering

  • Fixed 1280 × 720 logical canvas, scaled to fit the window with letterboxing.
  • Sharp on high-DPI screens: the backing store is scaled by devicePixelRatio.
  • Frame time is capped at 50 ms so the animation does not jump after the tab was in the background.

Deep links

Add ?pick=N (1–5) to start already holding instrument N, which is useful for testing:

?pick=1 · ?pick=2 · ?pick=3 · ?pick=4 · ?pick=5

Robustness & limits

  • Held notes are released when the window loses focus, so no notes get stuck.
  • Key auto-repeat is ignored, so holding a key does not retrigger the note.
  • Only one on-screen pad can be pressed at a time with the mouse or touch. Use the keyboard for chords.
  • On touch screens there is no right-click or Esc, so there is no way to put an instrument back.
